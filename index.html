RESEARCH IN PROGRESS

As device dimensions approach the sub-3 nm regime, nanoscale thermal physics becomes central to transistor performance and reliability.

My research develops a 3D atomistic model of a heterogeneous Gate-All-Around transistor to investigate thermal transport and self-heating beyond conventional bulk continuum assumptions.

02NEMD-based anisotropic thermal conductivity of confined nanosheets
